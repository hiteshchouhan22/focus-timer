Focus Timer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Focus Timer is a Pomodoro timer app developed by ShadowBox Studios. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data collection

Focus Timer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or non-personal data. The app works entirely on your device:

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No network access — the app has no internet permission and works fully offline
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind
  • No access to your contacts, location, files, camera, or microphone

Your timer state and completed-session count exist only while you use the app on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Third parties

Because the app collects no data, no data is shared with any third party.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released.
